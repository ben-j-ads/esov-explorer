Read this before you use the number

The 0.5pp figure is an average across categories. It comes from cross-category analysis. Any single category, and any single brand, can sit well above or below it.

Smaller brands and new launches typically need higher ESOV. A brand with a small share has to outspend its share just to get noticed, so the slope in this tool understates what a small brand needs to grow.

SOV here means share of category media spend. It is your share of the spend in the category, not impressions, reach, or the share of voice in any one channel.

The maintain band is an assumption. This tool treats ESOV within ±2pp as "maintain." That threshold is a choice for display, not a finding.
