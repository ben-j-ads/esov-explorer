# ESOV Explorer

An interactive chart of the excess share of voice (ESOV) rule: ESOV = share of voice − share of market, in percentage points.

Based on the IPA / Binet & Field finding that 10pp of ESOV drives roughly 0.5pp of market share growth per year. The tool predicts annual share change as ESOV × 0.05.

Open `index.html` in any browser. No build step and no dependencies.

- **Sliders:** current market share (1–60%) and share of voice (0–80%), where SOV is share of category media spend.
- **Chart:** ESOV on the x-axis (−20 to +40pp), predicted share change on the y-axis, the regression line through the origin, and your brand as a dot. Shaded zones mark maintain (within ±2pp of zero), grow, and shrink.
- **Readouts:** ESOV, predicted change per year, SOV needed to hold share, and SOV needed to grow 1pp a year.

The 0.5pp figure is a cross-category average. Smaller brands and new launches typically need higher ESOV. The ±2pp maintain band is a display choice, not a finding.
